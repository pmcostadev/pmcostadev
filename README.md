### `03 —` HOW I WORK

`PHASE 01` &nbsp; **Scope & architecture.** Blueprint and fixed-scope definition, so you
know what you get before we start.

`PHASE 02` &nbsp; **Live-code prototyping.** A clickable, working version of your site or
app, built conversion-first.

`PHASE 03` &nbsp; **Deployment & validation.** Fast, measured launch with tracking and
real-user feedback loops.

`PHASE 04` &nbsp; **Scale & evolution.** Automated follow-up that turns traffic into leads,
plus full handover or an ongoing partnership.
